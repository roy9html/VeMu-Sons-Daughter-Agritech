Mission

To empower farmers with high-quality seeds, reliable machinery, and expert guidance that enhance agricultural productivity and sustainability

Vision

To become a leading force in modern agriculture by fostering innovation, supporting local farming communities, and driving global food security through excellence in products and service.What makes us unique is our deep-rooted connection to the land and our commitment to integrating traditional farming wisdom with cutting-edge agricultural technology

History

Our journey is rooted in family and a deep passion for agriculture. It all began in 1990 when our parents, Nyaga and Weveti, founded a small farming supply business in Embu. With a strong understanding of the land and a heartfelt commitment to supporting fellow farmers, they laid the foundation for what would become a trusted name in the agricultural sector. Through years of dedication, honesty, and belief in the power of farming communities, Nyaga and Weveti steadily grew the business—from offering basic seeds to providing a full range of high-quality seeds, advanced machinery, and innovative farming solutions. Their vision was simple yet powerful: to help farmers succeed and to build lasting relationships based on trust and service. Today, the second generation—Murimi, Murithi, and Mumbi—proudly co-own and lead the business. Bringing fresh ideas and modern technologies while upholding the enduring values taught by their parents, they continue to drive the company forward. Together, as a family, we are committed to serving farmers across the country with passion, innovation, and the same spirit of excellence that Nyaga and Weveti started over three decades ago.
